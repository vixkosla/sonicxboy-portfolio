# Search semantics

The portfolio balances a strong authored identity with explicit commercial
search intent. Keywords belong in useful, human-readable copy; they are not a
separate hidden layer.

## Placement

- `<title>`: brand plus the two strongest specializations.
- Meta description: concise service summary written for the search-result click.
- H1 and SSR-visible reactor card: natural exact phrases with evidence and
  personality.
- JSON-LD `knowsAbout`: technologies and deliverables, not promotional claims.
- `public/llms.txt`: broader service vocabulary, working principles, demos,
  products, and contact routes for AI agents.

Do not add `meta keywords`, invisible keyword blocks, location lists, or
repetitive variants. Revisit this map whenever the offer or product portfolio
changes.
